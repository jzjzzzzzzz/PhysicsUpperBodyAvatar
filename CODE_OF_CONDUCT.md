# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Review code and evidence rather than
people. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats and publication of another
person's private information are not accepted.

Keep discussions relevant to the project. Do not submit patient-identifying
information or private recordings in issues, screenshots or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions or restrict
participation when needed to keep collaboration productive. Report sensitive
incidents privately using the security reporting link in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers will avoid sharing private reports unnecessarily and will explain
moderation decisions to the affected participants when practical.
